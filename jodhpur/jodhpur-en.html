Travel Information

Jodhpur Travel Planning
🌤️ Best Time to Visit: October to March is generally considered one of the more comfortable seasons for visiting Jodhpur.

🚗 Local Travel: For city sightseeing you can use auto, cab or a private vehicle. For routes outside the city, a private cab or car may be more convenient.

💙 Blue City: Wear comfortable footwear while exploring the narrow streets and old-city areas.

🏜️ Outdoor Routes: During hotter months, early morning or evening can be more comfortable for outdoor sightseeing.

⚠️ Important: Check current timings, entry fees and access conditions of attractions before travelling.

Stay & Food

Hotel • Restaurant • Local Food
🏨 Jodhpur City Hotels: Staying around the Blue City, Fort or central city area can make sightseeing convenient.
