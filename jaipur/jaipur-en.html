⭐ Jaipur Quick Travel Guide

🏰 Forts: Amber Fort, Jaigarh Fort and Nahargarh Fort.

👑 Palaces: City Palace and Hawa Mahal.

🛕 Temples: Moti Dungri Ganesh Temple, Birla Mandir, Galtaji and Govind Dev Ji Temple.

🛍️ Shopping: Johari Bazaar, Bapu Bazaar and the famous markets of Pink City.

📸 Photography: Hawa Mahal, Patrika Gate, Jal Mahal and Nahargarh.

🌅 Sunset: Nahargarh Fort is one of the best options for sunset views.

🍛 Rajasthani Food: Try Dal Baati Churma, Gatte Ki Sabzi, Ker Sangri and a traditional Rajasthani thali.

Jaipur Route Summary

How to explore Jaipur in limited time

If you have only 1 day, give priority to Pink City and Amber Fort.

If you have 2 days, keep Pink City for Day 1 and Amer + Jaigarh + Jal Mahal + Nahargarh for Day 2.

If you have 3 days or more, you can comfortably cover Galtaji, Birla Mandir, Patrika Gate and Chokhi Dhani as well.

⭐ Most Recommended Sequence

🩷 Pink City → 🏰 Amer Fort → 🌊 Jal Mahal → 🌅 Nahargarh → 🛕 Galtaji → 🌿 Central Jaipur → 🎭 Patrika Gate → 🏡 Chokhi Dhani
