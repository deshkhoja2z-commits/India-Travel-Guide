⚠️ Important Khajuraho Travel Note

Khajuraho sightseeing is best planned according to temple timings, weather and available daylight. Start your main sightseeing early so that you can comfortably cover the major monuments.

Keep drinking water with you, wear comfortable shoes and avoid rushing between places. For Panna and wildlife areas, check local timings and availability before planning your visit.

🎭 Khajuraho Culture

Khajuraho is famous for its magnificent temples, detailed stone sculptures and rich cultural heritage. The temples represent the artistic and architectural excellence of medieval India.

Apart from the temples, visitors can experience local traditions, handicrafts, food, festivals and the cultural atmosphere of Bundelkhand.

Respect local customs and maintain proper behaviour while visiting temples and heritage monuments.

⚠️ Final Important Travel Note

Plan your Khajuraho journey according to your available time. If you have only one day, concentrate on the major temple groups.
