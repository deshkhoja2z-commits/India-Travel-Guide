🏛️ Most Important Khajuraho Travel Point

The major temple groups of Khajuraho are located relatively close to each other. However, Panna Tiger Reserve, Raneh Falls and other nature destinations are outside the city.

Therefore, it is more convenient to first visit the Western, Eastern and Southern Groups of Temples and then travel to nearby nature destinations.

🚗 During road journeys, travel time may change depending on weather, traffic and local road conditions.
