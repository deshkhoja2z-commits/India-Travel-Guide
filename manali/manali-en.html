🚌
Bus Delhi → Chandigarh → Manali
🚕
Local Taxi Taxi for sightseeing and local travel
🚗
Car Rental Rental cars for local and long routes
🧭
Tour Guide Local guide for sightseeing
Important: Delhi to Manali is about 530–550 km by road. Chandigarh to Manali is about 300–320 km. Kullu to Manali is about 40 km.

Weather: Mountain weather can change quickly.

Snow: Snow conditions vary by season and location.

Roads: Check road conditions before travelling to Atal Tunnel and Sissu.

Safety: Drive carefully on mountain roads and follow local instructions.
