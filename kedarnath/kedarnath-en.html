🚗 Important Road Journey Information

Kedarnath Temple is not accessible by normal road vehicles. The practical road journey ends around the Sonprayag/Gaurikund area.

🚶 From Gaurikund, pilgrims continue towards Kedarnath Temple by trekking approximately 16–17 km.

Therefore, it is better to use separate Google Maps options for the Road Route and Trek Route.

📏 Distance Information

For the Kedarnath Yatra, it is important to understand the difference between road distance and trekking distance.

🛣️ The road journey from Haridwar/ Rishikesh towards the Kedarnath route can be roughly around 220–250 km, depending on the starting point and road route.

🚶 Plan for approximately 16–17 km trek from Gaurikund to Kedarnath Temple.

⚠️ Actual distance can change because of traffic, road conditions, diversions, weather and local transport arrangements.

⭐ How Many Days for Kedarnath?

📅 2-Day Tight Plan:
Day 1 — Haridwar/Rishikesh → Sonprayag → Gaurikund → Kedarnath
Day 2 — Darshan → Gaurikund → Sonprayag → Return

📅 3-Day Better Plan:
Day 1 — Haridwar/Rishikesh → Guptkashi/Sonprayag
Day 2 — Gaurikund → Kedarnath Trek → Darshan and Stay
Day 3 — Kedarnath → Gaurikund → Sonprayag → Return

📅 4–5 Day Comfortable Plan:
Haridwar/Rishikesh → Rudraprayag → Guptkashi → Sonprayag → Kedarnath → Return. This gives you more time for mountain travel and rest.
⚠️ Important Travel Information

🔱 Kedarnath is located at a high altitude of around 3,581–3,586 metres.

🥾 Consider your fitness and weather conditions before starting the trek.

🌧️ Rain, snowfall, landslides, traffic and road conditions can affect your travel plans.

🕐 Temple opening/closing dates, darshan arrangements, registration, helicopter services and other rules should be verified from official sources before travelling.
