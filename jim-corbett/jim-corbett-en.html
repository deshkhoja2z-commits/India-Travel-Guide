Travel Information

Jim Corbett Travel Planning
🐯 Best Time to Visit: Choose your travel season according to weather, safari availability and the current park opening rules. For safari, it is better to check permits and timings in advance.

🚗 Local Travel: Ramnagar can be used as a convenient base for exploring areas around Corbett. For local sightseeing, a cab or private vehicle can be useful.

🐯 Safari Planning: Jim Corbett has different safari zones. Zone availability, entry rules, permits and safari timings may vary. Check the latest information before booking.

⚠️ Important: Follow all forest rules and stay on the designated routes. Maintain a safe distance from wildlife and never feed animals.

Stay & Food

Hotel • Resort • Jungle Stay • Restaurant
🏨 Ramnagar Hotels & Resorts: Staying in or around Ramnagar can be convenient for visiting different parts of Corbett. While selecting a hotel, check its distance from your selected safari gate.

🌲 Forest Area Stay: Some forest accommodation options may be available according to forest authority rules and availability. Check current booking information and entry rules before planning an overnight stay.

🍽️ Food: Restaurants and cafes around Ramnagar and Corbett offer North Indian food along with local food options.

⭐ Stay Tip: If Dhikala safari is your main purpose, check the travel time to Dhangari Gate and the required safari reporting point before selecting your accommodation.

Safari Planning

Zone • Permit • Timing • Wildlife
🐯 Major Safari Zones: Bijrani, Dhikala, Jhirna and Dhela are among the well-known safari zones of Corbett. Availability and entry rules can differ between zones.

🎫 Permit: Before the safari, check permit requirements, ID requirements, entry gate and reporting instructions.

⏰ Timing: Safari entry and exit timings can vary according to season and forest department rules. Check the latest timing when making your booking.

📸 Wildlife Photography: Remain quiet and do not disturb wildlife. Tiger sightings are never guaranteed, so enjoy the complete forest experience rather than focusing only on spotting a tiger.
