⚠️ Important Travel Information

🏔️ High Altitude

Leh-Ladakh is a high-altitude region. Give your body enough time to adjust after reaching Leh and avoid rushing to higher destinations.

💧 Acclimatization

Take adequate rest, drink enough water and follow local or medical advice if you experience altitude-related symptoms.

🌦️ Weather & Roads

Weather and road conditions can change quickly in mountain areas. Check the latest local conditions before starting long road journeys.

📄 Permits & Local Rules

Some areas may have permit, registration or other local requirements. Check the applicable rules before travelling.

📱 Connectivity

Mobile and internet connectivity may be limited in remote areas. Keep important information available offline.
