Travel Information

Jaisalmer Travel Planning
🏜️ Best Time to Visit: October to March is generally considered one of the most comfortable seasons for visiting Jaisalmer.

🚗 Local Travel: For city sightseeing you can use auto, cab or a private vehicle. For Sam, Tanot and Longewala, a private cab or car is more convenient.

🐪 Desert Experience: Camel safari, jeep safari and desert camping may be available at Sam Sand Dunes. Availability can change depending on the season and operator.

⚠️ Important: Tanot and Longewala are border-side routes. Check current access rules, timings and road conditions before travelling.
